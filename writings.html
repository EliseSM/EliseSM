Dermatology RAG

August 2026

Using a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) approach, I built a dermatology question-answering system that can answer questions about skin conditions and treatments. The system retrieves relevant information from a curated vector database of PubMed abstracts and generates accurate and informative responses to user queries with citations. This project was used as a demo when I presented a RAG workshop on Medical Search using AI at the Computer Science Girlies Hackathon.

Turn any article, document, or webpage into a multi-host podcast — complete with audio. PodBot uses an AI agent to iteratively write, critique, and refine a two-host dialogue script (ALEX & SAM), then synthesizes it to MP3 using text-to-speech. May 2022

Animal face recognition is a new area of research within current facial recognition research. It is being developed in order to see if it is an adequate and potentially useful way to identify animals without needing to use alternative methods such as tagging or microchipping. Various facial recognition methods have been implemented on many different animal species in commercial, residential, and wildlife settings, and there is a wide range of potential different uses for this developing technology.
