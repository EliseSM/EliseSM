About Me

I'm an AI/ML engineer interested in building with emerging technologies, particularly agentic workflows and the integration of AI into real-world systems. I enjoy taking an idea from a quick prototype all the way to a production-ready product, and I'm especially excited by projects where I can combine machine learning, software engineering, and systems design to build something genuinely useful. I'm excited by opportunities to build AI systems from the ground up, especially where there's room to experiment, solve hard engineering problems, and turn new ideas into products people actually use.

Currently, I'm building RAG and agentic systems that help investigate cyber cases and generate reports for cyber analysts. I'm particularly interested in making AI systems more reliable, explainable, and practical, from building evaluation pipelines and improving context management to designing for resumability and developing the tooling needed to make these systems work well in production.

My experience at Capital One has given me the opportunity to work across the stack. I started out building backend transaction APIs that processed 600,000+ transactions per day before moving into cybersecurity, where I've worked across machine learning and data engineering. I've designed and productionized anomaly detection, data drift detection, and other large-scale ML systems focused on improving data quality, some of which now run across roughly 70% of Capital One's data.

Outside of work, I enjoy building side projects, making pottery, weightlifting, and crabbing. I'm usually happiest when I'm learning something new, building something from scratch, or getting my hands dirty, sometimes literally.
